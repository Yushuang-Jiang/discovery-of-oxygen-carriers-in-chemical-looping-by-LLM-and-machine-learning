Evidence:
The paper explicitly states in Section 2.2 that "XRD patterns are operated in the range of 20−80°". Figure 2 displays XRD patterns for fresh La0.6Ca0.4Mn1-yGayO3 samples. Figure 7d shows XRD patterns for La1-xCaxMn0.8Ga0.2O3 samples. Figure 9d shows XRD results for LCMG20-P and LCMG20-S. Figure 10c shows XRD patterns for LCMG20 after cycles.

Regarding TGA/TG/DTG/TPD/O2 evolution, the paper does not contain traditional TGA weight-loss curves. Instead, it presents "Real-time O2 production rate" and "total O2 production" profiles which serve as the functional equivalent for oxygen evolution analysis in this context. Figure 6a and 6b show O2 release profiles for La0.6Ca0.4Mn1-yGayO3. Figure 7a and 7c show O2 release for La1-xCaxMn0.8Ga0.2O3. Figure 8a and 8c show O2 release at different temperatures. Figure 9a shows O2 release for different fabrication methods. Figure 10a shows O2 release during cycle stability tests. The text explicitly mentions "O2 release start-up temperature", "O2 production rate", and "total O2 production" in the captions and body text associated with these figures. The paper notes in Section 3.4 that some literature uses indirect TGA, but this work measures directly via electrochemical sensors, confirming the absence of TGA figures for their own data while acknowledging the method in literature comparisons.
